# Lost-and-Found Mobile App — Personas

## Persona 1: The Student Who Lost an Item

**Name:** Brian Kiptoo  
**Age:** 21  
**Role:** University Student  
**Year:** 3rd Year  
**Technology Level:** High

### Background
Brian is a third-year university student who frequently moves between lecture halls, the library, cafeteria, and hostel. He recently lost his wireless earbuds on campus and wants a quick way to check whether someone has found them.

### Goals
- Report a lost item quickly.
- Search for items that have been found.
- Upload a photo and provide details about the lost item.
- Receive updates when someone finds a matching item.
- Submit a claim and recover his item.

### Frustrations
- Not knowing where to report a lost item.
- Searching through many unrelated posts.
- Having no way to track the status of a claim.
- Spending too much time describing an item.

### Typical Scenario
Brian loses his earbuds in the library. He opens the app, reports the item as lost, adds a description and photo, and searches the found-item listings. He later receives a notification that an item matching his description has been found and submits a claim.

## Persona 2: The Student Who Finds an Item

**Name:** Mercy Wanjiku  
**Age:** 20  
**Role:** University Student  
**Year:** 2nd Year  
**Technology Level:** High

### Background
Mercy regularly uses the university library and common student areas. One afternoon, she finds a student ID card near the cafeteria and wants to help return it to its owner without exposing unnecessary personal information.

### Goals
- Report found items easily.
- Add a photo and description of the item.
- Specify where and when the item was found.
- Help the rightful owner identify and claim the item.
- Avoid being contacted by unrelated people.

### Frustrations
- Not knowing where to hand in found property.
- Difficulty identifying the rightful owner.
- Receiving multiple false claims.
- Forms that require too much unnecessary information.

### Typical Scenario
Mercy finds an ID card near the cafeteria. She opens the app, selects **Report Found Item**, uploads a photo, enters the location and description, and submits the report. When someone claims it, the system allows the claim to be reviewed before the item is returned.

## Persona 3: The Lost-and-Found Administrator

**Name:** David Mwangi  
**Age:** 28  
**Role:** Student Affairs / Lost-and-Found Administrator  
**Technology Level:** Medium–High

### Background
David is responsible for helping students resolve lost-and-found cases. He needs an organized way to monitor reported items and verify claims instead of relying on WhatsApp messages, notice boards, or physical records.

### Goals
- View reported lost and found items.
- Monitor submitted claims.
- Verify whether a claimant is the rightful owner.
- Update claim/item status.
- Prevent fraudulent or duplicate claims.
- Keep records of resolved cases.

### Frustrations
- Unorganized reports.
- Fake or incomplete claims.
- Difficulty tracking the status of cases.
- Students providing insufficient item details.

### Typical Scenario
David receives a claim for a found phone. He checks the item's description and the claimant's information, verifies ownership, and updates the claim status to **Approved** or **Rejected**.
