# User Research – Interview Questions
## 1. Purpose of the Interview
   
The purpose of this user research is to understand students' experiences with lost and found items on campus. The interviews will help identify the problems students face when reporting, searching for, and claiming lost items. The findings will be used to guide the design of the Student Lost and Found Mobile Application.
---
## 2. Target Participants
The research will involve five university students who represent the intended users of the proposed mobile application.
---
## 3. Interview Questions
   
### A. Experience with Lost and Found Items

**Q1. Have you ever lost an item on campus? If yes, what happened?**
**Q2. What type of item did you lose or find?**
**Q3. Where did you lose or find the item?**
**Q4. Have you ever found an item belonging to another student?**
---
### B. Current Methods

**Q5. What did you do after realizing that you had lost an item?**
**Q6. How would you normally search for a lost item on campus?**
**Q7.Have you ever used WhatsApp groups, social media, friends, security offices, or other methods to find or return an item?**
**Q8. What challenges did you experience when trying to find or return a lost item?**
---
###C. Proposed Mobile Application

**Q9.	Would you use a mobile application for reporting and searching for lost and found items on campus? Why?**
**Q10.	What information would you expect to provide when reporting a lost item?**
**Q11.	What information would you expect to provide when reporting a found item?**
**Q132.	How useful would it be to upload a picture of a lost or found item?**
---
###D. Searching and Claiming Items

**Q13.	What search or filter options would be most useful to you?**
**Q14.	What information would help you determine whether a found item belongs to you?**
**Q15.	What information or proof should a person provide before claiming an item?**
**Q16.	What concerns would you have about someone falsely claiming an item?**
---
###E. Usability and Feedback

**Q17.	What would make the application easy to use on a smartphone?**
**Q18.	What would make the application confusing or difficult to use?**
**Q19.	How would you like to receive updates about your report or claim?**
**Q20.	What other feature would you like to see in the application?
**
---
##4. Research Approach
Five students will be interviewed individually. Participants will be identified using codes such as P01, P02, P03, P04, and P05 rather than their real names.
The interviews will focus on participants' previous experiences, current methods of handling lost and found items, expected application features, and usability preferences.
The responses will be analyzed to identify **common problems, user needs, and design requirements** for the proposed mobile application.

##5. Ethical Considerations
Participants will be informed that the interviews are for academic purposes. Personal information such as registration numbers, phone numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information will not be collected or included in the GitHub repository.
